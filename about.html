About FreeMart

A small, hand-maintained directory — not an automated scrape of "top 100 free tools" lists.

Why this exists

Most "free tools" roundups online are outdated, AI-generated lists that haven't been checked in months — half the links go to tools that now require a card on file. FreeMart started as a simple fix: a list of free tools that are actually still free, actually still working, checked by a real person before they're added.

How entries get picked

Every tool on FreeMart is tried firsthand before it's listed. We check three things: does the free tier actually do something useful, is a card required to start, and is the company still active. Entries are rechecked periodically — if a tool changes its pricing, it's updated or removed.

How FreeMart makes money

Mainly through affiliate commissions on select deals (see our Affiliate Disclosure) and, as the site grows, display advertising. Free tools remain free to use whether or not FreeMart earns anything from the link.
