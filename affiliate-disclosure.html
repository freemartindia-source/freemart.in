Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: September 2026

In accordance with disclosure guidelines for affiliate marketing, this page explains FreeMart's relationship with the companies it links to.

What this means

Some links on FreeMart — mainly on the Deals page, occasionally elsewhere — are affiliate links. If you click one and then sign up or make a purchase, FreeMart may receive a commission from that company. This comes at no extra cost to you; the price you pay is the same whether or not you use our link.

What this doesn't mean

  • It doesn't mean a tool is listed because it pays a commission — free tools with no affiliate program are listed on exactly the same criteria.
  • It doesn't give FreeMart any access to your personal or payment information — that transaction happens entirely between you and the provider.
  • It doesn't change the provider's price, terms, or service in any way.

How to tell which links are affiliate links

Pages containing affiliate links carry a visible note near the top of the page. As the directory grows, individual affiliate links may also be marked with a small "partner" label.

Questions

If you'd like to know whether a specific link is an affiliate link before clicking it, email hello@freemart.in and we'll tell you directly.
