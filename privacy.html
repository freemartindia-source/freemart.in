Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information FreeMart.in ("FreeMart", "we", "us") collects when you visit this site, and how it's used.

Information we collect

FreeMart does not require an account to browse the directory. We may collect:

  • Usage data — pages visited, approximate location (country/city level), device and browser type, via analytics tools such as Google Analytics.
  • Voluntarily provided information — your email address if you join the newsletter, or your name, email and message if you use the contact form.

Cookies

This site may use cookies for analytics (to understand how visitors use the site) and, once display advertising is enabled, for ad personalization via Google AdSense. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will still function, though some features may behave differently.

Third-party links

Most of FreeMart's value is in linking to external tools and services. Once you click through to a third-party site, that site's own privacy policy applies — we don't control, and aren't responsible for, how those sites handle your data.

Affiliate tracking

Some outbound links are affiliate links that use tracking cookies or parameters so the partner network can attribute a signup or purchase to FreeMart. This does not give us access to your personal information on the partner's site — see our Affiliate Disclosure for more detail.

Newsletter

If you sign up for the newsletter, your email is stored only to send you FreeMart updates. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email we send.

Data retention and deletion

We keep contact-form and newsletter data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for. To request deletion of your data, email hello@freemart.in.

Children's privacy

FreeMart is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site grows. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@freemart.in.
